Privacy

RideCue has no accounts. There's nothing to sign up for, and it doesn't know who you are.

On your phone

Always. Never sent anywhere.

  • The rides you star and the alert rules you set
  • Your trips and leave time
  • Light or Harbor, and your recent-alerts log

Kept on the phone, inside the app. Delete the app and it's gone.

On our server

Only if you turn alerts on.

  • A device token Apple issues for your phone — the address a notification is delivered to
  • The same alert rules, so the server knows when to send one
  • Which alerts already fired, so you don't get the same one twice

No name, email, phone number, or location. Turn alerts off and the record is deleted.

Where the numbers come from

Live wait times come from ThemeParks.wiki; the app asks it directly. Weather comes from Open-Meteo and lightning from Xweather, both fetched by our server.

There are no analytics, no ads, no trackers, and no cookies.
