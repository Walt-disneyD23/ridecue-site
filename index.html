Five tabs. One job each.

Live
Every wait in all four parks, refreshed every 3 minutes while parks are open, with what's hot and what's quiet right now.
Today
Your park day as a board: which rides to hit, in which order, from now till close. Nothing to enter.
Plan
Pick your dates, or ask when to go. Typical waits by park and season, from five years of days.
My Rides
Star a ride and it seats here with its live wait, its alerts, and its own rule.
Wrap
The day, wrapped every night: what ran, what went down, and how the waits moved. Weekly and monthly too.
